# Changes

## 1.1

- **Live previews** — protected windows' Stage Manager thumbnails and Mission Control tiles now look normal to you, while screenshots and recordings still show them black. Their animations play smoothly too. On by default; you can turn it off in Settings → General.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — pause and resume all protection, protect the window or browser tab you're using, and more. Set your own in Settings → Shortcuts.
- **Pause** now pauses everything at once, and resuming brings it all back.
- Better full-screen, browser and multi-display handling, and many smaller fixes.
- Now licensed under AGPL-3.0.
